## Required review for every model
- Confirm visual appearance and scale in Godot scene.
- Confirm category and origin/license evidence.
- Check material/texture and skeleton/animation behavior where relevant.
- Confirm Android import/runtime performance.
- Reviewer, evidence and explicit acceptance record.

License metadata currently lists CC0-1.0 candidate intake; this is not final license verification, visual acceptance or production approval.
